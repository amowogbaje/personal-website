Uniform and booklists

Uniform

Two sets of school uniform, black school shoes, white socks, a school bag, and sports kit (house colour T-shirt, shorts and canvas shoes). Nursery pupils also need a nap mat.

Books and stationery

A full booklist for each class is sent with your offer letter. Exercise books and stationery are sold at the school store each term.

First day checklist

Water bottle, packed snack or lunch plan, a labelled bag and change of clothes for Nursery.

Quick answers

How do I report an absence?

Use the message form on this page before 8am and choose “Report an absence”.

What if my child needs medication at school?

Send the medication in its box with a note, and tell the school nurse through the message form.

What time does school start and end?

Lessons run from 8:00am to 3:00pm. Early years close at 2:00pm.

Can I meet my child’s teacher?

Yes. Request a meeting through the message form or join the parent-teacher meeting on the calendar.

How do I get my child’s report card?

Report cards are issued at the end of each term at the parent-teacher meeting.

How do I pay fees?

Pay by bank transfer using the details on your offer letter, then send proof of payment from the fees page.

What happens if school closes unexpectedly?

We send notice on the parents’ WhatsApp group and update the calendar page.

Is there an after-school club?

Yes. Clubs run until 4:30pm and can be added on the fees page.
