About Ireti

A garden venue built for celebrations.

Space to celebrate properly

Our garden, hall and lounge host weddings, corporate galas and private celebrations, with a team on hand from setup to close.

  • Easy to reachMinutes from the Lekki-Epe expressway.
  • All in one placeCeremony, reception and after-party on one site.

Questions

How do I reserve a date?

Send an enquiry. A 50% deposit holds your date for 14 days.

Can I bring my own vendors?

Yes. Caterers and decorators are welcome, subject to a small vendor fee.

What time must events end?

Indoor events end by 1am and outdoor events by 11pm.

Is there parking?

Yes, secure parking for 200 cars with attendants.
