About Us

A trusted construction partner, committed to quality, innovation and timely delivery.

Our story

Projects by HAV was founded to give clients a construction partner they can trust from first sketch to final handover. We work with homeowners, developers and organisations who want clear communication, honest costs and work that lasts.

Projects by HAV Limited (RC 6951788) was incorporated in May 2023. We began by offering clients construction project management and execution services. Today our work also covers estate design and development, general project management, design and build, and project consulting.

Led by our founder, Ifeoluwa, and based in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, we are driven by a bold vision: to create the best and biggest cities in Africa, one dependable project at a time.

Mission and vision

Mission. To build structures, estates and cities that make our customers’ homes the most desired place to live, visit and invest.

Vision. To create the best and biggest cities in Africa.
