About Tolu

Thoughtful gifts, packed with care.

Gifting that reflects your brand

We pack hampers for companies that want to say thank you properly, from ten gifts to a thousand.

  • Curated, not randomEvery item is chosen to look and taste good together.
  • On brandRibbons, tags and cards in your colours.
  • On timePacked and delivered to your deadline.

Questions

What is the minimum order?

10 gifts for ready-made hampers and 50 for fully branded boxes.

How early should I order?

Two weeks for most orders and four weeks in December.

Can you deliver to individuals?

Yes. Send us a list and we deliver each hamper with a personal card.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes, we prepare a sample for orders above 50 gifts.
