The approach

Real color, shown in real light.

Nicole Marie Salon was built on a simple idea: every photo posted is unfiltered, so what you see booking an appointment is what you'll actually leave with. No ring-light color correction, no editing a brassy tone into something it isn't.

It's a small-salon approach to a big-salon industry — one chair, one client at a time, and enough attention paid to get the tone, the cut, and the finish right the first time.

Meet Nicole

Nicole Marie Salon is run by Nicole — stylist, colorist, and the person behind the chair for every appointment booked here. The salon's whole identity started from a habit: posting every result exactly as it came out, unedited.

That standard carries into the work itself. Consultations are honest about what your hair can do in one sitting versus over a few visits, and the goal on every appointment is a result that still looks right a week later, not just walking out the door.
