Questions we get asked

How far ahead should I order?

Give us 48 hours for most orders and a week for events above 200 guests.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about halal, vegetarian or allergy needs in your request and we will plan the menu around them.

Is there a minimum order?

The minimum is 10 guests for Boardroom Lunch and 20 for Smallchops.

Do you provide servers?

Servers and setup are included in the Event Buffet. For other packages they can be added.

How do I pay?

A deposit confirms your date and the balance is due on delivery.
